Contact about privacy

A useful enquiry, without sensitive details

Include a short description, the relevant website address and when the issue happened. A screenshot can help if you have first removed personal or financial information.

Do not send PINs, passwords, OTPs, card credentials, BVN, NIN or identity-document copies. Email buttons open your email app; you decide whether to send the message.

Three habits for safer payments

  • Keep your PIN, OTP and account credentials private.
  • Check the recipient or merchant identity and the amount before authorising a real transaction.
  • Use contact details published by the relevant provider rather than details supplied by an unsolicited caller or message.

If an actual bank account or card is at risk

Contact the bank or payment provider through its verified official channels. This informational website cannot freeze accounts, reverse transactions or take custody of funds.

For complaints concerning a CBN-regulated financial institution, contact that institution first and consult the CBN's published complaint guidance for the applicable escalation process.
