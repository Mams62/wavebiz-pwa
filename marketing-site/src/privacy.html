How this website handles information, how to contact us, and the choices available to you.

Privacy starts with a clear scope.

This notice covers the Wave Transakt marketing website and messages you choose to send to our published email address. The website does not open financial accounts or process payments.

1. Who is responsible?

WAVE TRANSAKT LTD is a Nigerian company, RC 9915202. For questions about this website or your information, contact wavetransakt@gmail.com.

2. Information involved

When you visit, the hosting service may process technical details such as your IP address, browser information and request logs to deliver and protect the website. If you email us, we receive the address, message and any information you choose to include.

The website has no financial-account registration form. It does not request your PIN, password, OTP, card credentials, BVN, NIN or biometrics. Please do not include these in an enquiry.

3. Why information is used

Technical information supports website delivery, security and fault investigation. Correspondence is used to respond to your questions, consider business enquiries and keep relevant records. These activities rely on the applicable lawful basis, including legitimate interests where appropriate; a separate explanation and choice will be provided for activities that require consent.

Sending an enquiry does not automatically subscribe you to a marketing list.

4. Service providers and sharing

Hosting and email providers handle the information necessary to deliver the site and correspondence. An email link opens your own email application, and your message is sent only when you choose to send it. Information may also be disclosed where required by law.

This website does not install advertising trackers or sell visitor information through a website feature.

5. Cookies, storage and video

The website does not add analytics or advertising cookies, and it does not save your walkthrough activity in browser storage. Browser or hosting functions may process information necessary for the site to work.

The current concept walkthrough runs within the page. If a third-party video is made available, playing it may send requests to that provider, whose own privacy notice and storage practices apply.

6. Retention and security

Correspondence is kept for the purpose of handling the enquiry and any applicable record-keeping requirements. Technical-log retention is also subject to the hosting provider's settings and obligations. You can contact us about deleting correspondence we hold, subject to any lawful reason for retention.

The public site is served over HTTPS. Avoid sending confidential financial or identity documents through ordinary website enquiries.

7. Your privacy rights

Under the Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023, applicable rights include access, correction, erasure, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent where processing relies on consent. Rights are subject to the conditions and exceptions in the Act.

Send requests to wavetransakt@gmail.com. We may need reasonable information to identify the relevant correspondence and respond securely. You may also raise a concern with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Product demonstrations

The customer name, wallet QR and ₦6,300.00 balance in the phone illustration are sample presentation content. They are not a customer-account record or a payment destination.
