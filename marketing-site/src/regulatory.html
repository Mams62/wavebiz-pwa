WAVE TRANSAKT LTD · RC 9915202

Incorporated in Nigeria on 6 October 2026. Wave Transakt is developing a QR-first customer and merchant experience, business-management tools and a separate POS experience.

1. Current service status

The public website introduces the platform and its roadmap. It does not open accounts, accept deposits, hold customer funds, perform identity verification or execute payments. Its sample profiles, wallet balances and QR patterns are illustrative.

Customer-app, Wavebiz and POS work is in development. Wallets, bank transfers, bill payments, cards and contactless features shown as planned are not an offer of those live financial services through this website.

2. Financial services and provider readiness

Regulated money movement is intended to depend on appropriately authorised financial institutions and provider arrangements. Required authorisations, contracts, verification, testing and settlement arrangements must be in place before relevant services are made available.

Company registration does not itself confer a CBN banking or payments licence. This website does not claim that its illustrative wallet is a licensed bank account or an insured deposit. Confirmed provider identities, applicable fees and limits, and relevant consumer-protection information will need to accompany any future live service.

3. Privacy and customer protection

The Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023 provides the national framework for personal-data protection, with oversight by the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Our website privacy notice explains the information involved in browsing this site or sending an enquiry.

For feedback, privacy requests or a concern about this website, visit Help & safety. A complaint about an actual regulated financial institution should first be raised through that institution's official complaints channel; the CBN publishes separate escalation guidance for institutions within its remit.

These official resources are provided for reference. They do not indicate an endorsement of Wave Transakt.
