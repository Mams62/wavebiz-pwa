# Wave Transakt branding

The website uses the existing black-circle and white-lightning-bolt identity from the Wave Transakt Android project, rather than the earlier three-wave placeholder symbol.

The icon's circle and bolt paths are preserved from:

- Repository: `Mams62/Wave_Transakt_android`
- Branch checked: `fix/bank-neutral-customer-ui-20261026`
- File: `app/src/main/res/drawable/ic_launcher_foreground.xml`
- Git blob: `6d908b70d8703004f4fcef09ca154dd807c4d9e8`

The black circular backing and the light “wave” above the bold italic “transakt” follow the established Android splash branding. The wordmark and icon are displayed together in the website's navigation, footer and walkthrough. The same icon appears in illustrative app screens, the browser favicon and video/sharing artwork.

`src/assets/wave-transakt-mark.svg` is the reusable vector mark. `src/assets/share-image.svg` is the editable sharing artwork; its PNG export is served as the social sharing image.
